Rescue USV — Remote-Controlled Life Buoy

Fast - Safe - Reliable

Overview:

  • Remote Control: Operate from a safe distance with no risk to rescuers
  • GPS Navigation: Precise positioning with return-to-home and waypoint navigation
  • Rapid Response: High-speed movement on water, faster victim reach than traditional methods
  • Self-Righting Design: Auto-flips if capsized, stable in rough water, marine-grade build
  • Long Battery Life: Extended operation time with quick-swap battery and power-saving mode
  • Two-Way Communication: Built-in speaker to reassure and guide victims, audio feedback for operators

Key Features:

Remote Control Operation

GPS Waypoint Navigation

ADVANTAGE

Advantages over traditional rescue methods

Deploys rapidly from shore or vessel, navigating water currents at speeds up to 15–20 knots to reach distressed swimmers in seconds.
Protects human rescuers by executing hazardous water rescue operations, handling rough seas and turbulent currents remotely.
Operates reliably through storm conditions, high waves, flood waters, and low-visibility night environments without fatigue.
Integrates high-precision GPS positioning and auto-hover technology to navigate straight to coordinates and stay in position.
High-capacity lithium battery system delivers continuous operation for extended missions with real-time battery monitoring.
Adaptable hull design operates in lakes, rivers, reservoirs, coastal waters, and flooded urban rescue scenarios.

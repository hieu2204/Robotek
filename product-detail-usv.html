Rescue USV — Remote-Controlled Life Buoy

Fast - Safe - Reliable

Overview:

  • Remote Control: Operate from a safe distance with no risk to rescuers
  • GPS Navigation: Precise positioning with return-to-home and waypoint navigation
  • Rapid Response: High-speed movement on water, faster victim reach than traditional methods
  • Self-Righting Design: Auto-flips if capsized, stable in rough water, marine-grade build
  • Long Battery Life: Extended operation time with quick-swap battery and power-saving mode
  • Two-Way Communication: Built-in speaker to reassure and guide victims, audio feedback for operators

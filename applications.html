Industrial Automation

Industrial Automation Systems

Zobotek power supplies deliver high reliability and low ripple noise for heavy-duty industrial machinery, PLC systems, and factory automation:

  • 24/7 continuous operation in harsh environments
  • Wide input voltage range (90–264V AC)
  • High surge protection (up to 4kV)
  • DIN rail & chassis mounting options
  • Active Power Factor Correction (PFC > 0.95)
  • Conformally coated PCB for humidity protection
  • Short circuit & over-temperature protection
  • Long MTBF (>300,000 hours continuous)

Operating temp: -30°C to +70°C
Ultra-low ripple noise (<100mV)
Rugged metal enclosure protection
